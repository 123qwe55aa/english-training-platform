# Learner rewards

Learners can earn configurable points for completing a video or confirming a dialogue exercise. Browser activity rewards are stored per learner and browser; they are motivational indicators, not identity-verified assessment records. Teacher awards and class progress are stored in the server database with teacher-provided evidence.

The meter shows low-energy, half-goal, goal, and over-goal charging states. The goal, low threshold, point values, and display label are configurable per learner. A celebration dialog appears when a new activity reward is earned. Respect the browser's reduced-motion setting.

Opening a page does not award points or mark a lesson complete. A video reward requires watching at least 90 percent and reaching the end; a dialogue reward is self-reported. Rewards do not assess speaking quality and are separate from teacher-confirmed class progress.
